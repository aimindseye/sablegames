# Sable Games 2.0.0

Seventeen keyboard-first games, each its own app, for BlackBerry-style Android phones such as the Zinwa Q25 and the Unihertz Titan 2. This release adds Gravel & Gears, a nonstop downhill mountain-bike game, and takes Bitter Melons from one world to four.

## What's new since 1.0.0

### Gravel & Gears (new app)
- **A nonstop ride:** the bike rolls on its own and you steer it across the trail. The ride gets a little faster with every gate.
- **Steering:** the trackpad, the arrow keys or I J K L steer, and holding a key keeps you turning. Up pedals harder and down brakes. Tilting the phone steers too (T turns it on and off), and on a touch screen you drag across the trail.
- **Cards are moves:** A S D F fire a card at once. Carves swing you across, hops lift you over boulders and logs, Tuck & Bomb speeds you up and doubles a gate, and Skid Stop brakes and clears mud. A dashed line shows where a move takes you, and the slot refills from your deck after about a second. Every third gate you add a new card to your deck.
- **Gates and combos:** ride through a gate for 100 points; each gate in a row raises the combo up to x8, and missing one resets it. Close calls past boulders and pines score too.
- **A new trail every ride:** gates, rock gardens, kickers, berms, mud and loose gravel land in new places each time. Kicker ramps are angled and always rise downhill, with a clear run-in and landing.
- **The rider is drawn from parts:** body, legs and wheels move smoothly every frame. The rider faces straight down the trail and leans only when you steer.
- **Three riders, all open from the start:** Gravel the goat keeps his combo off stumps, Spokes the fox earns free Redraws, and Knobby the badger smashes small rocks.
- **Wildlife:** marmots and deer by day, with crows flying over the trail.
- **Night Ride:** opens at gate 18 of the day trail. You see only a headlight cone ahead, the gates stay lit, and every score counts 1.5 times. Raccoons and stags cross the trail, owls swoop low (ride under them), skunks spray you if you brush past, and a swirl of fireflies gives +150 and three seconds of full headlight.
- **Between gates:** a panel after each stage, Retry from the last gate after a crash, and a short Ready count before each start.

### Bitter Melons
- **World 2, Slop Mall:** the Split Cantaloupe, which splits into three in the air, and gold AD banners that throw a melon off faster.
- **World 3, Server Foundry:** steel blocks, the Prompt Bot, the Heavy Honeydew that slams straight down, cooling fans that blow melons upward, and steel beams that drop when hit.
- **World 4, Storm Citadel:** the Overripe Melon that bursts in a juice shockwave, firewall shields that break or turn, and drone platforms that hold Slop in the air.

### Other games
- **Snakes & Ladders and Ludo:** the board fills the square under the top bar again.

## Install

Download the APKs you want below and open each one on the phone, or run `adb install sable-<game>-2.0.0.apk` from a computer. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists every file's SHA-256, so you can check a download with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt`.

- Android 11 or later.
- No permissions: no internet, no storage and no location. The only exception is the app-private one AndroidX adds for its own receivers.
- Everything works offline, with no ads and no accounts.
- Every APK is signed with the same key as TenSeven Launcher. Its certificate SHA-256 is `9b2047390e5aa77537b8e8875cdfd3c2c72d0a0715e172a7f77457fc4300f292`.

2.0.0 installs over 1.0.0 and keeps your scores and progress.

## Known limits

- Gravel & Gears and Bitter Melons have no sound yet.
- Lua console games run sandboxed: no files, no network, and limits on time and memory.
- The planned installer that sets up several games in one go is not part of 2.0.
