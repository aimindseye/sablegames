# Sable Games 2.1.0

Nineteen keyboard-first apps for BlackBerry-style Android phones such as the Zinwa Q25 and the Unihertz Titan 2. This release adds Hades: Tartarus Breach, a first-person arena roguelite, and the Sable Installer, which sets up the games you pick in one go.

## What's new since 2.0.0

### Hades: Tartarus Breach (new app)
- **Four Gates:** Asphodel, Phlegethon, the Labyrinth of Minos and the Abyssal Seal. Each has four timed waves and a Guardian: Cerberus, Talos, Asterion and the Echo of Kronos.
- **First person, full screen:** W A S D move, J L or the trackpad turn, K spins 180°, Space fires and Shift dashes.
- **Four artifacts:** Apollo's Sunbow (a flame streak), Poseidon's Trident, Hades' Bident and Zeus's Aegis Bolt, picked with Q W E R.
- **Ten foe types, each with its own way to fight:**
  - **Close in to strike:** Hoplites, Stalkers, Imps, Minotaurs and Titans.
  - **Strike from range:** Wisps, Harpies, Cyclops, Gorgons and Acolytes.
  - **Fliers:** Harpies, Wisps and Acolytes fly; the rest stay on the ground.
- **Boons between waves:** pick one of three. Some pairs combine into a stronger effect, and cursed boons trade max HP for power.
- **A gentle start:** Gate 1 starts with one foe straight ahead. Early foes walk straight at you, slowly and from the arc you face, and wait a few seconds after appearing before they attack. Melee foes stop in front of you and rest between swings. Stage 1 is melee only, early waves hit softer, and stages run longer.
- **Foe map:** a round map at the top right shows every foe around you. The foe that just hit you is marked in red, and the edge of the map flashes where the hit came from.
- **Settings on the Gate screen:** Difficulty (Easy, Normal, Hard), Aim assist (Off, Normal, Strong), Sound and Vibration.
- **Feedback:** short synthesized sounds (no audio files) and a vibration when you're hit.
- **The Shrine:** spend obols on lasting upgrades between runs.

### Sable Installer (new app)
- **Groups or single games:** pick a whole group (Board games, Card games, Lua games, Retro, Action and adventure, Puzzle and word) or single games.
- **Quick picks:** Not installed (N), Updates (U) and Clear (C).
- **Safe installs:** each download is checked against `SHA256SUMS.txt`, its package name and the Sable signing key before Android is asked to install it.
- **Manage installed games:** Open (O) and Remove (X); Remove opens Android's own app page.
- **Updates itself:** when a newer installer is out, it is picked automatically and installed last.
- **Reliable downloads:** they resume after a dropped connection, with three tries.
- **Wi-Fi only (F):** nothing downloads over mobile data.
- **Release notes:** the release's notes show at the top.

## Install

Install the Sable Installer, open it, allow it to install apps when Android asks, and pick the games you want. Or download the APKs you want below and open each one on the phone, or run `adb install sable-<game>-2.1.0.apk` from a computer. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists every file's SHA-256, so you can check a download with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt`.

- Android 11 or later.
- **Most games ask for no permissions:** no internet, no storage and no location. The only exception is the app-private one AndroidX adds for its own receivers. There are two exceptions:
  - **Sable Installer:** asks for `INTERNET` (to download from this release), `REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES` (to hand the APKs to Android's installer) and `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` (only for Wi-Fi only).
  - **Tartarus Breach:** asks for `VIBRATE`, for hit feedback.
- Games work offline, with no ads and no accounts.
- Every APK is signed with the same key as TenSeven Launcher. Its certificate SHA-256 is `9b2047390e5aa77537b8e8875cdfd3c2c72d0a0715e172a7f77457fc4300f292`.

2.1.0 installs over 2.0.0 and keeps your scores and progress.

## Known limits

- Gravel & Gears and Bitter Melons have no sound yet.
- Lua console games run sandboxed: no files, no network, and limits on time and memory.
- The installer needs this release's assets to keep their names (`sable-<app>-<version>.apk` and `SHA256SUMS.txt`).
